# Code of conduct

## The standard

Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagreement about technical
matters is welcome and expected; contempt for people is not.

Specifically, this project asks contributors to:

- Give and accept criticism about **the work**, not the person doing it.
- Say what you actually measured, including when it contradicts what you
  hoped. Reporting a result that undermines your own change is a
  contribution, not a failure.
- Be patient with people who know less than you about a part of this,
  which will at some point be everyone.

Not acceptable: harassment, personal or political attacks, publishing
others' private information, sexualized language or imagery, and
sustained disruption.

## Scope

This applies in project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions —
and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers by opening a private security
advisory (which is a private channel) or by contacting a maintainer
directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy,
and may temporarily or permanently block accounts for repeated or severe
violations. Maintainers who do not follow this policy in good faith may
face consequences decided by other maintainers.

## Attribution

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
shortened deliberately: a policy nobody reads protects nobody.
